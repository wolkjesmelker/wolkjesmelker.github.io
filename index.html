Open identification protocol

CSID Protocol
Callsign at Every Edge

Inspired by PTT-ID systems (MDC-1200), FSQ automatic preambles, and acoustic data modes like Ribbit and ggwave — CQ Tracker transmits your callsign as a compact 4-FSK burst at the start and end of every PTT press.

01
PTT Press → Prepend Burst

150–250ms sub-audible 4-FSK burst (22–34 Hz) encodes your callsign before voice audio begins.

02
Voice Transmission

Normal SSB/FM voice passes through. CSID tones are mixed at -22 dB relative to voice — near-inaudible to the human ear.

03
PTT Release → Append Burst

Trailing burst confirms callsign exchange. Receiver decodes via Goertzel tone detection + CRC-validated binary codec.

CQ Tracker-CSID-v1 FrameSpec
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PREAMBLE  0xAA          (8 bits)    │
│ LENGTH    3-12          (5 bits)    │
│ CALLSIGN  base-37×6bit  (per char)  │
│ CRC-8                   (8 bits)    │
│ POSTAMBLE 0x55          (8 bits)    │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ MODULATION: 4-FSK                   │
│ TONES: 22.5/25.5/28.5/31.5 Hz       │
│ BAUD: 40 symbols/sec (25ms/sym)     │
│ BURST: ~200ms total                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
Legal note: Amateur stations must identify per local regulations. CSID is an open, non-encrypted supplementary ID burst — not a replacement for voice identification. Always follow your licensing authority's rules (e.g. BIPT, FCC, Ofcom).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my audio ever leave my machine?

No. All audio processing (CSID decoding, waterfall, VOX) runs locally. CQ Tracker never uploads receiver audio to the cloud — the only network traffic is QRZ profile lookups, the CQ spot network, and license validation.

Will it work with my vintage receiver?

Yes. Audio-only mode needs nothing but receiver audio reaching your sound card — no CAT control required. For modern transceivers, Hamlib, FLRig, and OmniRig integration adds automatic frequency and mode tracking.

Is the CSID burst legal to transmit?

CSID is an open, non-encrypted, supplementary identification burst — comparable to PTT-ID systems used in commercial radio. It does not replace voice identification; you must still identify per your licensing authority's rules (e.g. BIPT, FCC, Ofcom).

How does licensing work?

Sign in with QRZ to verify your callsign. Each callsign can start one 14-day trial. CW and voice recognition can be previewed but are inactive during the trial. When the trial ends, the app requires an upgrade. Purchasing an upgrade will be added in a later update.

Which payment methods are supported?

Payments are not available in this test release. Upgrade details are visible throughout the trial; purchasing will be added in a later update. No payment or bank transfer is required to test the app.
