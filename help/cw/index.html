CW radio: one RX/TX conversation

Receive Morse, send supervised messages, adjust the radio knobs and save the exchange.

The CW page presents a radio-style console with one chronological conversation. RX and TX have distinct markers, colors and alignment, so you can follow an exchange without switching between separate text windows. Short receive pauses stay in the ongoing passage; a longer silence or an intervening transmission can start another bubble.

Receive first

Choose the actual audio input and press RX Start listening. Allow input permission when requested. Tune to a clear CW tone and check the RX level/tone strip. Auto-tune follows a detected tone; if several signals compete, use a stable selected tone and a narrower/cleaner radio passband. Adjust Gain for level and Sensitivity for detection. Excess gain can turn noise into false keying rather than improve reception.

The Monitor switch lets you hear received audio. Check decoded words against what you hear: fading, interference, uneven keying or a wrong tone can cause errors. WPM/SNR/lock status are diagnostic indicators, not guarantees of a correct callsign. Stop listening when changing input devices or finishing the receive session.

Send and tune

A supported connected CAT radio is required for the page's Send action. Enter a short message or insert a preset, inspect its text and resolve any unsupported-character warning. Set Speed in WPM, Tone in Hz and the local monitor level. The VFO knob and step buttons tune a connected radio; the CW button requests CW mode when available. Frequency memories are shortcuts, so verify the physical radio and the band before sending.

Drag a knob or focus it and use the arrow keys for controlled adjustments. Tone adjustment can preview the local sidetone. Press Send CW only when ready, watch the active text and use Stop to interrupt it. A stopped/failed bubble remains identified as such; do not assume the pending remainder was transmitted. A local sidetone is not proof that RF reached the antenna.

Understand the meters and save controls

The meters follow the app's live audio paths: RX reflects received audio and TX follows the local sidetone after its gain control. They are audio indicators in dBFS, not calibrated radio S-meter, SWR or RF watt readings. Lowering the local monitor level can reduce the TX meter while CAT still controls the radio. A quiet meter therefore needs a signal-path check before drawing a conclusion about RF output.

Save conversation saves the exchange; Save RX saves received text only. Clear RX removes RX text from this view while retaining TX. New session clears both directions and starts a fresh conversation: save first if you want to keep it. If you scrolled back, use the follow action to return to new messages. Enter the other station's callsign and open the logging form to record the actual QSO separately from the transcript.
