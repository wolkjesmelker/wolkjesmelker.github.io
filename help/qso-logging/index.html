Log a contact accurately

Review a station, complete the QSO form and distinguish a saved contact from a live detection.

Log a QSO after an actual contact has taken place. Incoming spots, decoded CQ messages and station lookups help prepare a contact but are not themselves confirmations that you worked the station.

Save a contact

  1. Open the station detail or Log action from your operating view. You can also use the logging control on the CW page after entering the other station's callsign.
  2. Verify every callsign character. Correct a detection or lookup result before it reaches the log.
  3. Check band, frequency and mode against the radio, particularly if you tuned since opening the form. Review the displayed date/time rather than assuming the computer clock is correct.
  4. Enter or confirm the reports sent and received, power and relevant notes. Use any available station-profile selection to describe the equipment actually used.
  5. Save, wait for confirmation and check the local log entry. Review a duplicate warning in context: the same station may legitimately be worked again on another band or at another time.

What happens afterward

Enabled synchronization destinations can receive the saved contact. If an upload fails, inspect its result without creating a second local QSO as a workaround. A digital exchange can also appear in the separate QSO transactions archive; this keeps its completed-exchange history even when the current activity list is cleared.

Use log filters carefully when finding a saved entry. Correct an existing record rather than adding another copy solely to repair a typo. Export the required ADIF/other supported records for interchange and retain a full backup before large imports or restores. A QSL card uses contact data from the log; inspect it before sending to avoid propagating an incorrect callsign or report.
