DigiWave Mobile

Operate your station from anywhere.

See live CQ and directed calls, tap to answer, and let your PC work the QSO and log it after 73 — all over the internet. Your station PC must be running CallScope HF with DigiWave and the rig connected.

Pair this device
  1. 1. On your PC, open CallScope HF → Settings → DigiWave Mobile.
  2. 2. Enable remote control.
  3. 3. Scan QR1 for phone or QR2 for tablet.
  4. 4. Log in once with your QRZ account.

The QR opens the pairing link automatically.
